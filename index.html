Product validation gates

Decide which AI ideas earn a build

Five gates, each with a stop rule and a time box set before the work starts. Effort matches the cost of being wrong. The output is a one-page evidence pack: build, stop, or test first.

How it fits: most teams already run an idea intake that captures data rights, pain, market size, pricing, and unit costs. These gates add three things on top: stop rules agreed before the work, an evidence pack for the decision, and a decision log that lets you measure the quality of every no.
